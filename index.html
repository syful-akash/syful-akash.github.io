Profile

Building Bridges Between
People & Learning

I am a curriculum and capacity development professional with over 6 years of experience designing and strengthening learning systems in low-resource urban settings across Bangladesh.

My work is rooted in evidence — from baseline assessments and field observation to feedback loops grounded in behavioural science. I have led training initiatives for government and NGO teachers, community volunteers, and literacy facilitators, always keeping learner wellbeing at the centre of every design decision.

Alongside education work, I bring a strong research background in physics and AI — including deep learning model development and Python-based applications — which informs how I approach data, systems, and technology in programme design.

I am also the founder of Helping Hand Organization, a community-driven initiative providing scholarships to 20–30 students annually in Barishal since 2018.
